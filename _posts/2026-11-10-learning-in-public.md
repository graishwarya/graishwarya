## Why I am documenting

Writing helps me organize what I understand, notice what I do not understand, and share useful ideas with others.

## A tiny publishing habit

1. Capture one question.
2. Learn and test the answer.
3. Write a short explanation in my own words.
4. Add one practical takeaway.
5. Publish and revisit later.

Small notes can become a meaningful portfolio over time.
