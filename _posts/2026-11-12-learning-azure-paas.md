## What PaaS means to me

Platform as a Service lets a team focus more on its application while Azure manages much of the underlying platform.

## Services to explore

- **App Service:** host web apps and APIs.
- **Azure Functions:** run event-driven code.
- **Azure Service Bus:** connect services through messages.
- **Application Insights:** observe application behavior.
