## Start with the problem

A feature request is not always the real problem. Before discussing a solution, ask:

- Who is experiencing the problem?
- What are they trying to accomplish?
- What happens today, and where is the friction?
- How will we know the outcome improved?

## From request to outcome

Instead of *“Build a new button,”* explore *“Help customers complete this task with fewer errors.”*

## My takeaway

Good product thinking connects customer needs, business value, and measurable outcomes.
